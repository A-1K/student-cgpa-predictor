Limitations & Notes
  • Dataset includes synthetic augmentation to reach n={{ n_responses }} from 53 original survey responses.
  • The Mean Baseline predicts the same average CGPA for everyone and should be beaten by useful ML models.
  • CGPA range in responses is narrow (2.0-4.0), which limits model variance explained.
  • All original data is self-reported and may contain response bias.
  • Findings reflect GIKI campus conditions and may not generalize elsewhere.
  • Negative or near-zero R2 indicates habits alone may not fully explain CGPA differences.
